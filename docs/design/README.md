# Preliminary screen designs

Open [screens.html](screens.html) directly in a browser. It is self-contained and makes no network requests.

It covers Pantry, Stock rules, Shopping, scan and batch sheets, product details, and reminder settings: navigation, search and location filters, quarter and exact-slice updates, rule thresholds, in-basket checks, simulated purchase confirmation, date labels (MHD, Verbrauchsdatum, No printed date, Not entered yet, Estimated reminder), and Track amount in.

Inventory cards are the chosen direction; the Rows and Compact alternatives remain behind Compare styles for reference. Grouping is shown with milk (2 cartons, about 1.5 left) and ground coffee (an opened pack plus an unopened spare that satisfies the spare-coffee rule).

Limits: sample data is fixed to 21 October 2026 and resets on reload; scanning is simulated with a sample milk product; the offline toggle only previews messages. Lookup, camera, permissions, notifications, persistence, and sync are specified in [the technical plan](../technical-plan.md).
